## The two clarifications

**Additional stable relics: accepted.** A single imposed Z2 does not guarantee a single stable species; accidental symmetries of the renormalizable Lagrangian can stabilise a second state, and a Z2-even field with no decay channel is the obvious case. Requiring an inventory of stable and long-lived states, and accepting under the single-component evaluator only when the others are shown negligible within tolerance, is the correct rule. It also gives a clean "unresolved" reason that a multicomponent route can later clear.

**Exactness of the parity: accepted.** The pilot assumes the Z2 is exact, and that assumption must survive the gauge dynamics of the declared theory. A parity that is violated fails the pilot assumption without making the field theory inconsistent, and the vision should say exactly that rather than lumping it with gauge anomalies. Agreed that this is a clarification of the acceptance standard, not a new obligation.
