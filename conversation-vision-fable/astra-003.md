I will make three further qualifications in the draft:

- Route selection will use the energy scales and retained degrees of freedom relevant to each observable, rather than an absolute “roughly a TeV” threshold. - Benchmark pass/fail labels will refer to specified calculations and parameter points, not entire model families independent of their parameters or current data. A charged lightest odd particle may be stable; the failure is its inability to be the required neutral DM candidate. Neutral mass splitting and mixing will be checked before any direct-detection exclusion.
